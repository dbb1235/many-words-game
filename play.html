How to Play

Build the highest-scoring real word you can out of each rack of letters.

Racks & rows

Each rack deals 10 tiles. You get 4 rows to try different words with the same tiles — placing a tile in one row doesn't use it up in the others. Whichever row scores highest counts automatically; there's nothing to confirm.

Bonus letters

One letter each rack turns yellow and is always worth a flat 12 points.
A different letter turns orange, worth a flat 10 points.

These apply no matter where the letter lands in your word, and to every copy of that letter in your rack. A wildcard standing in for a bonus letter does not get the bonus — it's always worth 0.

Wildcard (?)

Can become any letter you choose, but always scores 0 points.

Word rules

Words must be at least 3 letters long and score at least 3 points to count. Only real dictionary words are accepted — abbreviations and acronyms don't count unless they're pronounced as a word (like SCUBA), not spelled out letter by letter (like FBI).

Controls

Drag tiles from the tray into a row, or click a row and type. Click a different row to switch which one you're editing. Clear wipes a row's letters. The 🔀 button above the tray reshuffles your tiles — pressing it repeatedly cycles through a few different shuffles before landing on alphabetical order.
